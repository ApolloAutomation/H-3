Apollo H-3 Installer

Install or reinstall the H-3's ESPHome firmware from Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  • Smart (Wi-Fi) connects to Home Assistant: change songs and LED effects, run automations, use the Bluetooth proxy and get firmware updates.
  • Basic (No Wi-Fi) is the firmware the ornament ships with: the buttons play the built-in songs, with no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

Before you install

  1. Take the batteries out of the ornament.
  2. Hold the boot button on the back, plug a USB-C data cable into the ornament and your computer, then let go of the button. (Boot mode guide)

After you install

  1. Unplug the USB cable and plug it back in so the ornament restarts normally.
  2. Smart firmware only: connect the ornament to Wi-Fi. Click Connect above again and enter your Wi-Fi details, join the Apollo H3 Hotspot network from a phone, or set it up from Home Assistant over Bluetooth (Improv via BLE). Home Assistant then discovers the ornament.

Factory reset: with the ornament awake (press a song button to wake it), hold the boot button for 10 seconds or more, then release it. This erases the Wi-Fi details and every saved setting.
